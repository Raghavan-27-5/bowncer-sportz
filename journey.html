The Coach's Story

The Journey

From a district trial in Avadi to an RCB dugout, a TNPL crease, a film set, and cricket grounds across six American states — trace the arc that built the coach behind Bowncer Sportz.

Chapter One

Foundations

Madurai to Chennai · Early Years
Where the grind began

Long before RCB or the TNPL, cricket was a discipline learned the hard way — chasing selection trials alone, and switching divisions on purpose to master a format nobody had assured him would pay off.

He told his family the school was taking him to trials. He told the school he was going home. Then he took a train alone to Avadi — and got selected.

He never played First-Class or List A cricket. Instead, he dropped from the first division to the second, on purpose, to give one format — T20 — his complete focus, training on a strict routine most players wouldn't commit to.
